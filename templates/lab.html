☀️ 1. Light Reflection

Healthy leaves absorb red light for energy and reflect near-infrared (NIR) light like a mirror. When trees are cut down, infrared reflection drops dramatically.

🌿 2. The NDVI Greenness Score

We calculate (NIR − Red) / (NIR + Red). High scores near 1.0 indicate thick green forest canopy; low scores near 0.1 indicate bare ground or stumps.

⚠️ 3. Automated Alert Trigger

When the computer compares two dates and sees NDVI drop by 20% or more, it flags those pixels in red. This drives ForestWatch's risk scores and ranger alerts.
