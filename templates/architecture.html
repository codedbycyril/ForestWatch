A CONNECTED APPROACH

From local signal to informed action.

ForestWatch brings people, satellite observations, and ranger teams into one practical response loop.

01⌖

Community reports

Citizens share suspected activity through a simple web form or SMS, including a location and optional details.

WEB · SMS
02◉

Satellite context

The system finds the nearest forest and checks its satellite vegetation change (NDVI) for additional context.

FOREST · NDVI
03⌁

Risk prioritization

A risk score from 0 to 100 combines satellite loss, activity type, distance, and nearby reports.

SIGNAL · SCORE
04↗

Ranger response

Rangers review a prioritized queue and can send alerts by SMS in English or Kiswahili.

REVIEW · RESPOND
THE PLATFORM UNDER THE HOOD

Built on practical foundations

The core technologies that connect field information to coordinated action.

FW / 01
FoundationHow it appears in ForestWatch
Hardware & softwareSatellite sensors, servers, and phones provide the hardware; the Flask app, SQLite, and browser provide the software layers.
DatabasesSQLite tables forests, incidents, and dispatches are linked by foreign keys (schema.sql).
NetworksHTTP/REST connects browser and server, while an SMS gateway calls the inbound webhook.
Data representationSatellite pixels are processed as NDVI = (NIR − Red) / (NIR + Red), a vegetation-change indicator.
Input & outputInputs include satellite sensors, keyboards, touchscreens, and feature-phone keypads. Outputs include the ranger dashboard and SMS alerts.
